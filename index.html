SNOWE.GPT ver. 0.5 [BETA]

SNOWE is a chatbot that is definitely, 100% made of snow. Do not leave it in the sun.

Ask him to code something and he writes it live in a code box. When he's done you can copy it, download it, or run it (HTML and JavaScript).

Shortcuts: / jumps to the textbox, Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a new line. Double-click the big title to rename a chat.

Chats are saved in your browser only.
